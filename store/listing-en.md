# English store listing

## Title
Rift Rush

## Short description
Aim the cannon, multiply your army, and smash the rift's castles.

## Full description
Rift Rush is a cartoon crowd runner. Hold to aim a cannon, send your army down the lane, and smash the castles that keep the rift open.

Green gates multiply your crowd. Red gates shrink it. Turn soldiers into archers, give them a shield, or forge a giant from ten of them. Six levels cross three biomes: meadow, canyon, and snow. Each level has up to three stars.

The daily challenge gives everyone the same rift. Break the castle in as few shots as you can. If you set a nickname of at least two characters, that name and your shot count can appear on the daily board. Shorter names stay on your device, and the rest of the game plays offline.

Rewarded ads play only when you tap for extra coins or ammo. A short ad may appear between later levels, never during a run and not in the first three levels. You can refuse ads in Settings. No in-app purchases.
